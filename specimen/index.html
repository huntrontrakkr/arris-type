Arris

A typeface grown from the Chamfer clock figures of the Dymaxion watch face: a drawing on a module grid, one rule for corners, and nothing drawn by hand that the rule can generate. At the SemiBold weight with Watch at 100, its figures are the watch's figures, pixel for pixel.

Clock

12:45

A colon between figures becomes the watch's time colon (calt). Figures are tabular by default, so the time never shifts as it changes.
