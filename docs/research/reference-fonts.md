# Reference fonts: measured optical corrections

Twenty-four open-licence faces, measured to calibrate Arris's corrections.
They fall into four groups:

* neutral sans: Roboto, Inter, Jost, Montserrat, Barlow, Atkinson
  Hyperlegible, B612;
* chamfered or octagonal faces: Chakra Petch, Oxanium, Orbitron, Aldrich,
  Michroma, Tomorrow, Saira, Rajdhani, Turret Road, Bai Jamjuree;
* monospaced faces: Share Tech Mono, B612 Mono, JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex
  Mono, Roboto Mono, Space Mono, Red Hat Mono;
* Roboto Flex, for its grade axis.

**Method** (`measure/measure_fonts.py`, `measure/geom.py`). Each glyph is
decomposed, overlap-removed with skia-pathops and flattened to within 0.01
units. Thicknesses are ink intervals along a slice. A diagonal's thickness
is the cut perpendicular to the stroke's fitted centre line through its
middle. Every number is a median over several slice positions. When the
spread across positions passes a threshold (contrast, taper, curvature),
the measurement is flagged. `measure/selftest.py` checks the method on
synthetic shapes with known answers.

**What Arris takes from it**

* **Horizontals:** 0.87–0.90 of the stem at Regular and 0.85–0.87 at Bold,
  chamfered faces included. Arris runs from 0.90 at Thin to 0.85 at Black.
* **Diagonals:** 0.94–0.97 of the stem. A chamfered bowl's 45° corner is
  close to the mean of its side and top. Arris uses 0.94, thinning to 0.84
  at Black so tight figures keep their counters.
* **Overshoot:** the chamfered faces' median is zero. Arris does not
  overshoot its octagonal bowls, and its blur model agrees.
* **Crossbars:** at 0.50–0.51 of the cap height in chamfered faces. Arris
  keeps the watch's geometric middle.
* **Grade:** Roboto Flex's grade keeps every advance fixed and moves a
  Regular stem by about ±30% across its range, less at Bold. Arris's grade
  is ±20% of the stem at every weight, also with fixed advances.
* **Vertical metrics:** x-height about 0.74 of the cap height, descender
  0.25–0.33. Arris uses 0.75 and 0.25.
