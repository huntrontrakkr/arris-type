Arris is a chamfered geometric sans with an industrial character and a cursive italic. Its strokes are monoline and every outer corner is cut at 45 degrees. Its figures began as the pixel numerals of a watch face designed for the Pebble smartwatch, and the rest of the family was developed from them.

The italic is slanted ten degrees, with every chamfer turned into a curve, a single-storey a and a descending f. The grade axis changes the thickness of the strokes without changing the width of any letter, so text can be made lighter or darker without reflowing.

Arris covers Latin, including Vietnamese, as well as Cyrillic and Greek, and includes arrows, mathematical symbols, box drawing, block elements and braille.

Variable axes:

  • Grade (GRAD)
  • Weight (wght)
